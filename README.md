## `01 // PROFILE`

I’m Abdul Muhaymin Nawaz, Founder & CTO of [The Technostics Group](https://technosticsgroup.com/).

I build at the intersection of **cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, software engineering, systems design, and next-generation computing**.

My work focuses on turning difficult ideas into practical systems—from security applications and intelligent detection tools to custom firmware, developer utilities, and experimental human-computer interfaces.

I’m interested in technology that is:

- Proactive instead of reactive
- Explainable instead of opaque
- Secure by design instead of secured as an afterthought
- Useful in the real world instead of impressive only in a demo

> Every system has assumptions.  
> Every assumption creates an attack surface.

## `02 // THE TECHNOSTICS GROUP`

**The Technostics Group** is my cybersecurity-focused technology startup, centered on practical security engineering, modern digital infrastructure, and useful technology for a changing world.

The organization explores:

- Cybersecurity products and services
- Application and infrastructure security
- AI-assisted security research
- Developer and automation systems
- Experimental computing and emerging interfaces
- Responsible security education and research

The long-term objective is to build technology that helps people and organizations understand risk before that risk becomes an incident.

**Website:** [technosticsgroup.com](https://technosticsgroup.com/)

## `03 // ACTIVE SYSTEMS`

### FRAUDTRACE CALL

An Android-first security concept designed to help people recognize potentially fraudulent calls through real-time warnings and explainable analysis.

- Bengali, Hindi, English, and Hinglish support
- Scam-tactic detection and risk indicators
- Research into AI-generated and cloned-voice signals
- Privacy-conscious analysis
- Transparent explanations instead of unexplained verdicts

**Repository:** [Fraudtrace](https://github.com/jker24256-max/Fraudtrace)

### CTF COMPANION

An AI-assisted cybersecurity learning and CTF environment focused on helping users understand challenges, investigate evidence, and develop structured problem-solving workflows.

- CTF-focused analysis and assistance
- Cloud-based reasoning with a local application architecture
- Exploration of HexStrike MCP integration
- Controlled and authorized security testing
- Emphasis on learning the methodology, not just receiving answers

### PROJECT AETHER

An experimental wearable-input project exploring a gesture-enabled ring as an interface for a personal computing workspace.

- Hybrid gesture-detection architecture
- Hands-free interaction concepts
- Software-first prototyping
- Optional NFC functionality
- Exploration of contextual and wearable computing

### TTG CARDPUTER

Exploring custom firmware and a more complete operating-system-like experience for the M5Stack Cardputer ecosystem, with potential applications in portable utilities and personal technology.

### AURUM AI

An experimental AI-driven quantitative research and paper-trading concept for Indian and US equity markets.

- Quantitative forecasting research
- Auditable signals and model evaluation
- Paper trading before any live-trading consideration
- Exploration of AI infrastructure
- Research into open-source models and responsible automation

> **Project status:** These projects are ongoing or exploratory. Features, implementation status, and technical scope may evolve.
